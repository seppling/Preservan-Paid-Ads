Preservan · Meta lead intake and booking · 2026 YTD

Meta leads book about 25 points below other sources. Slow response alone does not explain it.

983 contacts created Jan 1 to Oct 7, 2026. A booked lead is one in Assessment Scheduled or any later status. Time to deal is the closest available measure of how fast a lead is booked, because first-contact times are not tracked.

Key insights
Booking gap

Meta books 60%. Other sources book 81 to 88%. Meta is 46% of lead volume (455 of 983), so the gap affects the largest share of leads.

Speed

A typical Meta lead gets a deal in 3.4 hours, but 1 in 4 takes more than 4.7 days. Weekend leads take 17 hours at the median against 1.4 on weekdays, and evening leads take 12 to 14 hours.

Caution

Slower response has not lowered the booking rate. Meta leads created on weekends and evenings book at 59.8%, the same as weekday daytime leads at 59.7%. Speed is a hypothesis here, not a proven cause.

Stalled leads

93 Meta leads are stuck (53 New Lead, 30 Cold, 10 Pending), with about 9 touches each. Another 27 are out of territory, which is an ad targeting issue.

Upside

If intake is the driver, 5 to 15 points of lift is worth $17K to $50K year to date. That uses the $1,427 Meta ticket and a 52% close rate from HubSpot status.

Blind spot

First-contact speed cannot be measured. Only 66 of 2,174 contacts have a first-call date. Without it, no one can prove or rule out an intake problem.

Recommended next steps

  • Test a local first touch. Send Meta leads a text from a local number within 5 minutes, with a booking link, and compare booking rate over about 3 months.
  • Log the first touch. Ask corporate to record the first call or text, with a timestamp, on every lead.
  • Cover weekends and evenings. Use an automatic text with a booking link, or extend CSR hours.
  • Work the stalled leads and fix targeting. Review the 93 stuck Meta leads, and ask the agency to tighten geography to cut out-of-territory leads.
  • Confirm two HubSpot definitions. What sets the marketing contact flag, and what New Approved Quote means. Both affect reporting.

  • Cutoff. Raw hours are heavily skewed (skewness 7.0). A log-scale outlier test flagged nothing, because the data is nearly symmetric once logged (skewness 0.13), so the tail is the natural shape. I used a Tukey upper fence on raw hours: Q3 + 1.5 × IQR = 45 + 1.5 × 45 = 113 hours (4.7 days).
  • Other cutoffs. A 7-day cap gives a pooled median of 0.58 hours, the 90th percentile 0.64 and the 95th percentile 1.07. The conclusion does not depend on the exact cutoff.
  • What trimming means. The 120 trimmed deals are mostly booked or quoted customers who took longer. Trimming describes the typical lead and hides the slow quarter, which is why that quarter is shown separately.
  • Time to deal as a proxy. A deal is created within a minute of the Customer change for 85% of leads that have both, so Customer is treated as assessment booked. It is not time to first contact.
  • Instant deals. Many near-zero times are contacts created already booked. Excluding anything under 5 minutes, the trimmed Meta median is 8.3 hours.
  • Meta definition. All 455 Paid Social leads count as Meta, including 116 flagged non-marketing in HubSpot, which is likely a tagging or cleanup effect (89 of them were created Jan to May). The agency's 467 matches Paid Social plus Organic Social plus Other Campaigns (455 + 6 + 6).
  • Cost. $27K YTD spend assumed to be all Meta gives about $59 per lead, about $99 per booked assessment and about $191 per approved or invoiced lead.
  • Call data. 66 contacts have a first-call date and 17 were created since March. 41 of the 66 had the call logged within a minute of creation, likely inbound calls that created the contact. For the 15 called within 7 days, the median is 1.5 hours, and the deal followed within 4 to 16 minutes in 6 of them. Too few to measure speed to contact.
  • Booking status. Lead status is a snapshot. Cancelled assessments and declined quotes are not separate statuses, so the booking rate may be slightly low.
  • Sources. 2026 Contacts export, Filtered Contacts 2 export and Jobber quotes export. 5 emails and 51 phone numbers are duplicated and were not de-duplicated.

Preservan Athens · Lead intake and booking · 2026 YTD · reconciled

Almost half of Meta leads never get an address captured. Once one is captured, 93% book.

43% of 2026 contacts belong to other territories and are excluded below. That leaves 559 leads. A booked lead is one in Assessment Scheduled or any later status. Meta is identified from the Source field, not Original Traffic Source.

Key insights
Data fix

424 of 983 contacts are from other territories (Charlotte, Jacksonville, Birmingham and others). They rose from about 5 a month in Q1 to 157 in July, and 93% sit in booked statuses. They explain the July lead spike and the inflated booking rate.

Booking gap

In-territory Meta books 50% (182 of 366). Agency web search books 96% and referrals 91%. Leads with no source recorded book 49%.

Where it breaks

176 Meta leads (48%) never had a city captured, and 5 of them booked. They are New Lead, Not Interested, Cold or out of territory. The 190 with a city book at 93%.

Speed

A typical Meta lead gets a deal in 2.2 hours, against 0.1 hours for other sources. Weekend Meta leads take 14 hours. But weekend and evening leads book at 47%, against 53% on weekday daytime, which is not a significant difference.

Revenue

Meta is 37% of converted revenue and about two-thirds of leads. Meta ticket averages $1,470 and the median is $1,243, about the same as other sources ($1,280). Meta earns $241 per lead against $504 for web search.

Upside

5 to 15 points more Meta bookings is worth about $5K to $16K year to date, at $291 per extra booking. The gap is smaller than earlier estimates, which used other territories' bookings.

Recommended next steps

  • Fix territory routing. Ask corporate to assign contacts by city or ZIP, and to confirm none of your ad spend is generating other territories' leads. 194 of the 424 are Meta-sourced.
  • Capture location first. Add the city or ZIP to the Meta form, and to the first text and call, so out-of-territory and unqualified leads are identified immediately.
  • Work the stalled leads. Triage the 50 Meta New Lead and 29 Cold Lead contacts that have no city, and remove those out of territory.
  • Test a local first touch. Text Meta leads from a local number within 5 minutes, with a booking link, and compare the booking rate over about 3 months.
  • Fill the Source field and log first touches. 141 of 2026 contacts have no Source, and first-contact times cannot be measured.

  • Territory rule. A contact is in territory if its city has at least 2 quotes in the Athens Jobber account (30 cities, mainly Athens, Lawrenceville, Dacula, Bogart, Watkinsville). 23 cities with one quote are ambiguous and 220 contacts have no city. Contacts in any other city are other-territory. Other-territory leads book at 93% and none match a Jobber quote, which fits a hand-off to another franchise.
  • No-city leads stay in. City is usually captured when an assessment is booked, so removing no-city leads would inflate the rate. They are counted as in scope. Some may belong to other territories, so the 50% Meta booking rate is a floor.
  • Source mapping. Meta is Source = Meta - Form, Meta (StructureM) or Meta - Call In. Original Traffic Source labelled 110 of the 560 Meta-tagged 2026 contacts as Direct, Offline or other. Contacts with no Source (141) stay unattributed.
  • Trimming. Time to deal is heavily skewed. I used a Tukey upper fence on raw hours, Q3 + 1.5 × IQR, which is 109 hours (4.5 days). Jan and Feb deals were created in bulk later, so they are excluded.
  • Time to deal as a proxy. A deal is created within a minute of the Customer change for 85% of leads that have both. It is not time to first contact, and only 66 contacts have a first-call date.
  • Cost. If the full $27K was spent for this territory, that is $74 per in-territory Meta lead, $148 per booked assessment and $450 per converted job (60). Converted Meta quotes total $88K, about 3.3 times the spend, before costs.
  • Agency count. I could not reproduce the agency's 467 leads from Source tags. Source gives 560 Meta-tagged contacts across all territories, or 366 in territory.
  • Booking status. Lead status is a snapshot, and Cancelled assessments and declined quotes are not separate statuses.
  • Sources. Filtered Contacts 3 export, Jobber quotes export, and earlier contact exports. 5 emails and 51 phone numbers are duplicated and were not de-duplicated.
